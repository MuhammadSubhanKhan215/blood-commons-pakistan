Why The Blood Commons?

Connected Ecosystem

Real-time connection between donors, hospitals, and blood banks — replacing fragmented phone calls and spreadsheets.

Intelligent Matching

Matches donors by blood type, geographic proximity, and clinical urgency — getting blood where it's needed, faster.

Safety & Transparency

Verifiable tracking from donation to transfusion ensures quality, prevents fraud, and builds trust.

Retention & Impact

Gamification and impact metrics keep donors engaged. Our pilot achieved 73% retention.
